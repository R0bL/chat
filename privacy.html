Privacy Policy

Robert Lalani · Family Check-In SMS Program · Effective October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Robert Lalani ("we", "us") collects and uses information in connection with the Family Check-In SMS Program (the "Program"), a personal, non-commercial text-messaging service that sends daily check-ins, reminders, and safety tips to consenting family members, and replies to messages they send.

Information we collect

  • Mobile phone number of each participant who has opted in.
  • Message content: the text messages sent to and from the Program number, including timestamps.
  • Delivery information provided by our messaging provider, such as whether a message was delivered.

We do not collect payment information, government identifiers, passwords, or account numbers, and the Program will never ask for them.

How we use it

  • To send and receive the Program's text messages.
  • To generate replies, which may be produced with the help of an automated assistant (an AI language model) that processes message content for that purpose only.
  • To keep a short message history so replies stay in context, and to produce a daily summary for the participant's designated family contact.
  • To alert the designated family contact when a message suggests the participant may be the target of a scam or may need help.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Who we share it with

Message content and phone numbers are processed by the service providers needed to run the Program: a telecommunications provider that delivers the messages (Twilio) and an AI provider that helps generate replies (Anthropic). These providers process the data only to provide their services. Mobile opt-in information and consent are never shared with any third party or affiliate for marketing or promotional purposes.

Retention

Message history is kept on a private server controlled by Robert Lalani and is deleted when a participant leaves the Program or on request.

Your choices

Reply STOP to any message to leave the Program at any time. Reply HELP for assistance. You may also request that we delete your information by contacting us.

Children

The Program is intended for adults and is not directed to anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.

Contact

Robert Lalani, operator of the Family Check-In SMS Program. Reply HELP to any Program message, or contact the designated family contact directly.
